01 — The platform

Trophy assets, made liquid.

Real estate has always been the world’s largest store of wealth, and the hardest to access. Blockstar removes the walls.

STEP 01

Acquire

Blockstar sources and acquires institutional-grade real estate, beginning with five-star hospitality in the world’s most sought-after destinations.

STEP 02

Tokenize

Each asset is represented on-chain through the Blockstar token on Solana, and large assets can be tokenized into individual asset-based tokens of their own.

STEP 03

Distribute

Property cash flow, after all costs, is distributed every quarter: 70% to token holders and 30% to the investor group.
